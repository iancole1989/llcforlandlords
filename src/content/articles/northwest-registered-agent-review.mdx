## Northwest Cons — What's Frustrating

The credibility section. Be honest.

### 1. BOI filing is a separate fee (~$25)

The federal BOI filing under FinCEN's Corporate Transparency Act is **free at fincen.gov/boi**. Northwest charging $25 to do it for you is a value judgment, not a scam — but most users don't realize until checkout.

If you're forming the LLC for the first time and want one less thing on your plate, $25 is reasonable. If you're operationally inclined, file it yourself in 30 minutes at fincen.gov.

(Note: BOI rule status was litigated multiple times in 2024-2025. Verify whether the rule applies to your specific situation before paying anyone to file it.)

### 2. The website UI is dated

Functional but not pretty. Compared to ZenBusiness or Tailor Brands, Northwest looks like 2015. The dashboard works, the forms work, but there's no slick onboarding wizard or branded color palette.

This doesn't affect the quality of the underlying service. It does affect first impressions, especially for users who associate UX polish with quality. For an operator who cares about the substance over the look, it's a non-issue.

### 3. No live chat — phone or email only

If you prefer chat-first support, Northwest isn't that. Phone support is fast (1-2 ring answer time during USA business hours) and email is reasonable (4-24 hour response). But there's no live-chat widget on the site.

For most questions, phone is faster than chat anyway. For users who specifically want chat, ZenBusiness has it.

### 4. EIN service is $50 extra

You can get an EIN for free in 10 minutes at irs.gov/ein. Northwest's $50 EIN service is paying for the convenience of not doing it yourself. For first-time filers who are intimidated by IRS forms, the $50 buys peace of mind. For everyone else, DIY at irs.gov.

We'd argue the EIN service is the most "value-questionable" Northwest add-on. The IRS form takes 10 minutes and is genuinely simple. Skip the upsell.

## Northwest for REI-Specific Patterns

The use cases where Northwest specifically earns its place over ZenBusiness, LegalZoom, or DIY.

### Wyoming holding LLC + property-state foreign registration

A common pattern for privacy-conscious investors: form a Wyoming LLC (anonymous, low fees), have it own membership interests in property-state LLCs, then foreign-qualify the property-state LLCs as needed. This is two-state filings — the WY formation + the property-state foreign qualification (or formation + foreign qualification for the holding structure).

Northwest handles both filings. They originated in WY, they know WY filings cold, and they have RA registration in all 50 states for the property side. ZenBusiness can do this; LegalZoom can do this; both with more friction than Northwest. For investors specifically pursuing this structure, Northwest is the operational default.

### Series LLC formation in TX, DE, IL

For portfolio investors using a series LLC structure: master LLC files standard articles, plus a series-specific filing in states that require it. Northwest has experience with all the major series-LLC states (Texas, Delaware, Illinois, Nevada, Wyoming). They handle the master + series filings cleanly.

A note on series LLCs: the structure is powerful for portfolio investors but financing-complicated. Many lenders prefer a single LLC per property over series-titled property because the lender's collateral is cleaner. Talk to your lender (or [CloseIron](https://closeiron.com/quiz/dscr) for DSCR-friendly options) before committing to a series structure for properties you'll finance.

### 1031 exchange entity formation

For 1031 exchanges into LLC-titled investment property, the LLC must be formed before the exchange identification period ends. Timeline matters. Northwest's standard processing handles 1031 timing in most states — state processing time is typically the constraint, not Northwest's filing speed. For tight 1031 timelines, expedited state filing (additional state fee, $25-200) gets the formation done within the 45/180-day windows.

### DSCR loan readiness

DSCR loan underwriters request specific documents at underwriting:
- Articles of organization
- Operating agreement
- EIN confirmation letter
- Member list with ownership percentages
- BOI filing confirmation (if applicable)

Northwest provides clean copies of all of these via the online dashboard. The dashboard's organization makes the lender requests easy — pull the docs, send to the lender, done. For operators applying for DSCR loans frequently (as serious REI do), the document organization saves real time.

### Multi-property portfolio onboarding

For investors forming multiple LLCs (one per property, or master + series), Northwest's pricing scales reasonably: $39 + state fee for each formation, $125/yr RA per LLC. ZenBusiness's pricing also scales but the RA cost ($199/yr each) adds up faster across a portfolio. For a 5-LLC portfolio over 5 years, Northwest is roughly $1,800 cheaper in cumulative RA fees than ZenBusiness Pro.

## Switching from Another RA to Northwest

A common scenario: you formed your LLC with LegalZoom, ZenBusiness, or another service, and the registered agent renewal is due. You want to switch to Northwest for the better pricing and privacy. The process:

### 1. Sign up for Northwest's standalone RA service

$125/yr. The signup happens on Northwest's site — they handle the next steps.

### 2. File a change-of-RA form with your state

Most states have a simple change-of-registered-agent form. State filing fees range $0-$100 depending on state:
- Texas: $15
- Florida: $25
- California: $30
- Wyoming: $5 (or free in some cases)
- Delaware: $50

Northwest will prepare and file this for you on your behalf — that's part of the standalone RA signup. Some states require the new RA to consent to the appointment (Northwest signs the consent automatically).

### 3. Cancel your old RA

Once the state's confirmation comes back (typically 5-15 business days), cancel your previous RA renewal. Don't cancel before the state has confirmed the change — you cannot have zero RA.

### 4. Update internal records

Update your operating agreement (the registered agent is named there in most templates), update any lender-facing entity records, and update your LLC's records of authorized agents.

The whole process is roughly 30 minutes of your time and 1-3 weeks of elapsed state processing. Worth it if you're saving $100+ per year on RA fees.
